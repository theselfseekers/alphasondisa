About

Thobelani Sondisa Mdubeki

I’m an architecture graduate (2021) based in Gqeberha. I design and visualise buildings with one question in mind: how will people feel living and working here?

My long-term focus is resort and environmentally responsive architecture. Today I help architects, builders and homeowners communicate their projects with clear drawings and realistic 3D images.

Good to know

Software: ArchiCAD, SketchUp, Twinmotion.

Drawings issued for municipal submission are prepared for review and signed by a SACAP-registered professional. I offer drafting and visualisation services.
